# Optical closure and round LG outlet baseline — 2026-09-16

This baseline promotes the round LG outlet after geometry, optical-path and GUI checks. It adds an optional precision executable built from the same implementation as the normal simulation.

## Geometry and optical behavior

- Keep total LG length at 30 mm and preserve inlet, outlet, gel, window and sensor positions.
- Unite a 29.9 mm tessellated taper (terminal radius 7.499 mm) with a radius-7.5 mm, length-0.2 mm cylinder. Their axial overlap is 0.1 mm. They form one LG physical volume.
- The exact circular outlet closes the mismatch between the former polygon outlet and circular gel. The taper slope and small cylinder-start shoulder are modeling approximations, not measured machining dimensions.
- Close the tile/foil perimeter with Al rims, excluding existing material volumes.
- Add absorbing tape sleeves around noLG gel/window sides and LG inlet-gel sides. Thickness 0.05 mm, direct contact, PVC substitute bulk and R=T=efficiency=0 are explicit idealizations.
- Make the LG outlet sleeve contact the radius-7.5 mm sensor stack; preserve the scintillator foil air gap.
- Preserve the existing LG/world AluminumSurf and LG/gel dielectric border surfaces. Union interiors are not separate material boundaries. The exposed shoulder receives the LG/world Al boundary treatment.
- Preserve physical termination and sensor response. No world-entry kill or production step-limit kill was added.

## Validation scope

Performed on the KNU CERN LCG_105 environment (Geant4 11.2), with e+ 60 GeV at (0,0,-100) mm along +z unless otherwise stated.

- Round-tip on/off: 21 physical placements, 20,000 overlap samples each, no overlap findings; 7,248 navigator checks and 256 outlet rays passed. The 30 mm overall extent and coupling interfaces were checked.
- Boolean visualization polyhedron generation succeeded with 11,888 facets. The user confirmed interactive GUI operation after fixing the trial working-directory QE-data link.
- Exposed gel-front seam probes: 20/20 world exits with the polygon outlet, 0/20 with the round outlet. Internal axial probes traversed the union and reached gel without an artificial internal boundary.
- Round-tip-off seed43 reproduced the preceding polygon-model canonical ROOT digest; noLG control also reproduced its preceding result.
- Precision observer counters for shoulder interactions matched full-step records in both assemblies. Default promotion and observer changes preserved the earlier round-tip trial canonical ROOT count/time digest at seed42.
- One selected photon was replayed for all 24 steps, including a T2 shoulder reflection at step17 and final QE rejection, with unchanged canonical ROOT digest.

Latest default-model campaign: seed44, five events per mode, single worker, complete photon summaries and validated ROOT output:

32 unique LG photons (0.03423%) encountered the exposed cylinder-start shoulder from inside LG: 30 reflection encounters and 2 absorption encounters. These are a whole-campaign denominator and encounter counts, respectively; targeted-probe reflection fractions are not population fractions.

This is a limited geometry and tracking validation, not proof of zero leakage at every beam condition or timing-resolution equivalence. Geometry changes alter random-number consumption and photon production; matching seeds across geometries do not create photon-paired samples. No absolute real-PMT prediction is certified. Large raw runs and personal working notes are not included in this code snapshot.

## Reproduce diagnostics

From the repository root after loading the local environment:

```bash
source envset.sh
cmake -S . -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DCBDsim_BUILD_PRECISION=ON
cmake --build build --target CBDsim CBDsimPrecision -j4
python3 CBDsim/precision/run.py --binary build/CBDsim/precision/CBDsimPrecision --out /tmp/round-lg-new --mode lg --seed 44 --events 5 --max-tracks 1000000 --timeout 600 --root
```

Use a new output directory for each invocation; the runner refuses an existing directory. For noLG, use `--mode nolg` and a different output directory. Complete photon summaries include final fates and shoulder counters; selected full-step tracing is optional.

For a GUI launched inside `build/CBDsim`, ensure its relative `analysis/reference/pmt_r2076/R2076_quantum_efficiency_percent.csv` resolves to the repository data. Existing local builds may have an `analysis` symlink; a fresh build must establish this data path before firing photons.
